FOREWORD
The Universe of Astro
The universe of Astro is built upon the conflict and coexistence of five great Houses. Each is not a nation nor a
race, but a philosophy of relationship with Astro — the rare substance found only within the Void, the most
coveted resource in the known galaxy.
They struggle for control over what is mined only in one place — and each interprets the substance through its
own lens. None of the five are wholly righteous, and none are wholly evil. Each carries a Light side (public,
noble, ceremonial) and a Shadow side (hidden, ruthless, true). It is a spectrum of moral grey — in the tradition
of Game of Thrones and Dune.
Beyond their endless quarrels, a greater shadow looms — The Architects, the ancient creators of Astro itself,
slowly returning from a forgotten age. None of the five Houses know they are coming. Only the Ophus Order
suspects something stirs in the deep.

HOUSE I
FEDERATION OF SOLAR SYSTEMS
Federatio Solaris · The Federation
ARCHETYPE
Stark + Atreides + Star Trek Federation
ORGANIZATION
Political alliance of planetary systems
COLORS
Sky Azure (#4A90E2) (Light) · Deep Navy (#1A2F5A) (Shadow)
PHILOSOPHY
Guardians of order and scientific knowledge. They were the first to systematise the extraction of Astro from the
Void and to establish protocols for its safe handling. They believe that access to Astro must be regulated,
lawful, and just — for without order, civilization will tear itself apart in a war for the resource.
This is the most sympathetic of the five Houses — the 'good guys' of the universe: diplomatic, democratic,
defenders of the weak. But sometimes too cautious. While they deliberate and debate, others act.
RELATION TO THE VOID
They study and regulate extraction. The Void is sacred and dangerous, demanding respect and protocol. The
Federation maintains the strictest safety regulations for working with Astro of any House.
■ THE LIGHT SIDE
Parade orbital fleets, white ships with crystalline solar sails, diplomatic protocols, open scientific forums,
democratic assemblies. The aesthetic of NASA + Apple + Star Trek bridge — clean, noble, optimistic.
SCIENTIA · CONCORDIA · PROGRESSIO
Knowledge · Concord · Progress
■ THE SHADOW SIDE
What hides behind the façade of order: covert intelligence networks, classified operations, hidden surveillance,
black ships of observation. They see everything — even when they say they do not interfere. The truth of
Federation power is the shadow it casts.
VIGILIA · SILENTIUM · IMPERIUM
Vigilance · Silence · Dominion
THE EMBLEM
A seven-pointed radiant star within a thin precise circle, bisected by a horizontal line. Rendered in metallic
silver-gold.
● Star — Helios, the source of light and knowledge
● Circle — order, protocol, the unbroken cycle
● Horizontal line — the horizon of the Void, which they guard and study
● Stylistic principle: symmetry, fine lines, nobility, no sharp angles

HOUSE II
HOUSE VORKHAN
Domus Vorkhan · House Vorkhan
ARCHETYPE
Lannister + Harkonnen + Khorne (WH40K)
ORGANIZATION
Bloodline dynasty / military clan
COLORS
Crimson (#DC143C) (Light) · Blood (#5C0000) (Shadow)
PHILOSOPHY
A brutal warrior dynasty. They take Astro by force. They believe only the strong are worthy to touch the source
— the weak deserve no voice in its fate. Their mines yield more than any other House because they do not
flinch at losses, their own or others'.
The most tense of the Houses — but not evil. They live by a code of honour, ancestral nobility, fierce loyalty to
clan. These are not mindless barbarians. They are aristocrats of war — like Vikings, like ancient horse-lords
made cosmic.
RELATION TO THE VOID
They conquer access to the Void. Their philosophy is simple: those who can hold the source by force deserve
it. They raid the deposits of other Houses, seize them, fortify them, defend them with iron will.
■ THE LIGHT SIDE
Parade armies, noble banners of warrior brotherhood, the aesthetics of knightly honour, rites of initiation, the
warrior's code. The heroic side of war — like samurai epics. Parade ceremonies in the craters of dormant
volcanoes, banners, ranks of gleaming weapons.
PUGNA · HONOR · IMPERIUM
Battle · Honour · Imperium
■ THE SHADOW SIDE
Bloody rituals, dark pacts, the cult of war without limits, the training of berserkers. Where Light shows the
parade of honour — Shadow reveals the bloody rites of initiation, ritual ordeals, dark oaths sworn by torchlight.
This is the true practice of war, hidden behind the parade façade.
MORS · PACTUM · VINDICTA
Death · Pact · Vengeance
THE EMBLEM

● Fist — strength, grip, dominion
● Obsidian — black glass born of the Void (a hint of raw Astro)
● Cracked — they sacrifice themselves for power; scars are badges of valour

HOUSE III
MERCANTI VERIS
Mercanti Veris · 'The True Merchants' · The Mercanti
ARCHETYPE
Hanseatic League + Spacing Guild + Free Cities of Essos
ORGANIZATION
Intergalactic merchant guild (multi-species)
COLORS
Gold (#D4AF37) (Light) · Bronze (#6B4423) (Shadow)
PHILOSOPHY
A multi-species trading guild. Not bound to any one nation or planet — they are a brand, beneath which
races from many worlds have gathered. They have endured for millennia, surviving empires and revolutions,
because trade is needed by all.
Their philosophy: commerce is above politics. They sell Astro to everyone — the Federation, House Vorkhan,
anyone who pays. They follow an ancient code of merchant honour — hence Veris (the True). A contract
sealed by the Mercanti is sacred; to break it is to be hunted to the ends of the galaxy.
Morally grey. Not 'good' nor 'evil' — pragmatic. Charismatic anti-heroes.
RELATION TO THE VOID
They have no mines of their own. Instead, they own the logistics — they carry Astro between Houses. Their
power lies in their monopoly on the routes. If House Vorkhan conquers the entire Void, they still need the
Mercanti to deliver it.
■ THE LIGHT SIDE
Open orbital trading stations, golden interstellar routes, parade negotiations on neutral grounds, diplomatic
agreements. The architecture of cosmic merchant palaces, transparent contracts — the public face of the guild.
LUCRO · FIDES · ITINERA
Profit · Faith · Pathways
■ THE SHADOW SIDE
Smuggling routes through hidden jump points, dark deals in orbital shadows, double agents, secret vaults, trade
in forbidden goods (information, biological forms of Astro, Keeper intelligence). On the surface — a flawless
guild. Beneath — a complex network of shadow commerce.
AURUM · UMBRA · CONTRACTUS
Gold · Shadow · Contract

HOUSE IV
THE VERGENT
Ordo Vergens · from 'verge' — the edge · The Vergent
ARCHETYPE
Bene Gesserit + Greenseers + Avatar Pandora + Annihilation
ORGANIZATION
Order of origin / biomancers
COLORS
Verdant Green (#00A86B) (Light) · Forest Moss (#1B3A1B) (Shadow)
PHILOSOPHY
Biomancers who treat Astro as living matter. They do not mine Astro in the traditional sense — they cultivate
it. They have learned to grow Astro within living organisms through symbiosis.
The name Vergent means 'those who stand on the edge' — between life and Astro, between nature and
science, between human and something more.
The most enigmatic faction. Neither evil nor good — other. Their motives elude the others: do they value life,
evolution, or something deeper that no one else can yet perceive?
RELATION TO THE VOID
Unique. They descend into the Void not to extract but to gather specimens — crystalline forms of Astro which
they then plant in their biospheres. From these specimens grow hybrid forms — plants and creatures, part
Astro, part living. This yields less resource, but purer and stranger.
■ THE LIGHT SIDE
Bioluminescent gardens on their home worlds, healing laboratories, hybrid plants, symbiotic creatures, the
aesthetic of living beauty drawn from Astro. Here the Vergent are life-savers, cultivators of wonders.
CONIVNCTIO · EVOLVTIO · UNITAS
Connection · Evolution · Unity
■ THE SHADOW SIDE
Infected jungles, root-vaults of forbidden specimens, mutations beyond control, eyes upon the branches,
parasitic forms, biological experiments on captives. Here the Vergent are not evil — but they play with fire,
and sometimes the fire wins. This is where symbiosis becomes absorption, and cultivation becomes distortion.
RADIX · OBSCURUM · GENESIS
Root · Darkness · Genesis
THE EMBLEM
A tight geometric spiral coiling inward to a small almond-shaped eye at its centre.
● Spiral — DNA, growth, evolution, life, inward draw
● Eye at the centre — living consciousness watching from within; a hint that their Astro looks back

HOUSE V
THE OPHUS ORDER
Synodus Ophus · The Ophus
ARCHETYPE
Bene Gesserit + Mentat + Lovecraft cult + Doctor Strange order
ORGANIZATION
Mystical religious order
COLORS
Amethyst (#9966CC) (Light) · Violet Void (#2A0040) (Shadow)
PHILOSOPHY
Mystics who use Astro for mental connection and prophecy. Their great temple stands on Ophus Prime.
They believe Astro is a language through which something speaks to them from the depths of the Void.
They descend into the Void not to extract but to commune. Many of their ranks change from this — gradually
becoming not quite human. They are the strangest of the Houses, but the most powerful in matters of the mind.
Unlike the others, the Ophus Order suspects something of The Architects. They hear whispers, see dreams,
receive visions. They do not know what is approaching — but they feel that it is coming.
RELATION TO THE VOID
The deepest and most dangerous of all. They descend into the Void further than any other. They listen, they
hear, and sometimes they answer. Their priests (the Augurs), after long meditation in the Void, often return
with information that should not have been knowable.
■ THE LIGHT SIDE
Enlightened halls of the order, libraries of secret knowledge, meditative rituals, golden scrolls of prophecy,
oracular practices, the aesthetic of noble mysticism. Here the Ophus are sages, advising the Federation,
interpreting dreams, helping others find meaning.
VERITAS · OCCVLTA · TRANSFORMATIO
Truth · Hidden · Transformation
■ THE SHADOW SIDE
Occult communion chambers, rituals of direct contact with the Void, dark knowledge, the cult of the listeners.
Here the Ophus are those who have gone too far. They are no longer fully human. They know what should not
be known. And they serve something that has not yet arrived.
ABYSSUS · MYSTERIUM · DOMINATIO
Abyss · Mystery · Dominion

● Inverted triangle — the gate, the descent into the Void, the path to the source

VII
The Greater Threat
THE ARCHITECTS
The makers of Astro. The reason none of the five Houses are truly safe.
An ancient civilization, vanished from the galaxy thousands of years before humans first reached the stars. They
created Astro — perhaps as a tool, perhaps as a byproduct, perhaps as a weapon. None of the five Houses know.
Now, slowly, they are returning.
Only the Ophus Order suspects something. The whispers in the Void grow louder. The dreams of their seers
grow sharper. Yet even they do not comprehend the scale.
STORYTELLING NOTES
The Architects are the final antagonist of the saga. They reveal themselves gradually, edition by edition, never
directly named in Edition II. Only hints in the Transmission Logs — a word, a coordinate, an unexplained
symbol.
When the five Houses inevitably realize the truth, they will face an impossible choice: continue their ancient
quarrel, or unite against something none of them can defeat alone.
This is the long-term arc of the Astro universe. It belongs to the future. For now — let the five Houses rise.
