# Part 2 — Storyboard (shot-by-shot)

19 clips in this exact order. Each house = sigil → environment → figure. Captions are optional on-screen text.

# Part 3 — Before You Open CapCut

Animate all 19 stills in Higgsfield (Kling 3.0). Use ~5–6 sec each, sound OFF, std mode to save credits. Approve each job in the Higgsfield interface.
Motion prompt for environments + crystal + Void + Architects + logo: “Slow, steady cinematic push-in. Fine luminous dust drifts gently. Subtle light shimmer. Slow majestic pacing, no fast movement, no zooms.”
Motion prompt for the five sigils: “The emblem slowly materializes and pulses with light, fine particles drifting around it. Very subtle slow rotation. No fast movement.”
Download all 19 clips to your phone or computer. Also have ready: your voiceover audio file, one music track, and your ASTRO logo PNG (transparent background).

# Part 4 — CapCut, Step by Step

Works the same on phone and desktop. Desktop is easier for a 2-minute piece — recommended.

## A. Set up the project

New Project. In the export/canvas settings choose aspect ratio 9:16 (vertical).
Import all 19 clips, your voiceover, and your music into the media panel (drag them in, or Import button).

## B. Lay down the clips

Drag clips onto the timeline in the storyboard order (1 through 19). They snap end to end.
Trim each clip to its target length: tap the clip, drag its right edge. Use ~5 sec for sigils, ~6 sec for environments/figures, per the storyboard times.
Add transitions: tap the small square between two clips, pick a transition. Use a fast “Flash” or “Glow” into each house’s sigil; use “Dissolve” between the other shots. Keep transitions short (~0.3s).

## C. Add the voiceover + music

Drag your voiceover onto an audio track under the clips. Line up each line with its shot (the storyboard timecodes are your guide). Nudge clips to fit the narration if needed.
Drag the music onto a second audio track. Lower its volume to about 20–30% so the voice stays clear (tap track → Volume).
Fade the music: tap the music track → Fade → add a short fade-in at the start and fade-out at the end.

## D. Add the captions (house names + mottos)

Text → Add Text. Type the house name + motto from the storyboard. Place it over each house’s sigil shot.
Pick a clean bold font, white or the house color. Add a subtle fade-in animation (Animation tab) so text doesn’t pop harshly.
On the final clip, place your ASTRO logo PNG: import it, drag it as an overlay onto the end-card, center it, add a fade-in. Add the tagline text below.

## E. Export

Export → Resolution 1080p, Frame rate 30fps. Confirm aspect is 9:16. Export and you have your CEO sample.

# Quick Tips

If 2 minutes feels long for the CEO, you can cut each house to sigil + 1 shot and land around 75 seconds. Same material, faster edit.
Music choice matters more than people think — pick something slow, epic, building. Search CapCut’s library for “epic cinematic trailer” or “cosmic ambient”.
Keep on-screen text minimal — house name + motto is enough. Let the visuals and narration carry it.
Total beginner? CapCut’s own tutorials cover every button above. This edit uses only the basics: timeline, trim, audio, text, export.
